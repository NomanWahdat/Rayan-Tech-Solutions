# Complete Guide: Starting a Software House in Afghanistan From Zero

## Important Note

This guide is educational and business-oriented. It is not official legal, tax, or accounting advice. For final decisions about company registration, taxation, contracts, and licenses in Afghanistan, you should speak with a qualified local lawyer, accountant, or government business registration office.

## 1. What Is a Software House?

A software house is a business that builds software products and provides software services.

A software house can build:

- Websites
- Mobile apps
- E-commerce platforms
- Business management systems
- School/university systems
- Hospital systems
- Accounting systems
- CRM systems
- ERP systems
- APIs and backend systems
- Dashboards and admin panels
- Custom software for companies

A software house can work in three ways:

### 1. Service-Based Software House

You build software for clients.

Examples:

- A shop needs a website.
- A school needs a student management system.
- A company needs an internal dashboard.
- A restaurant needs an online ordering system.

### 2. Product-Based Software House

You build your own software product and sell it.

Examples:

- Your own e-commerce marketplace
- Your own POS system
- Your own school management SaaS
- Your own accounting software

### 3. Hybrid Software House

You provide services to clients and also build your own products.

This is usually the best model for you because you already have BazarAF as your own product, and you can also earn money by building software for clients.

## 2. Can You Run Your E-Commerce Project Under the Software House?

Yes, in many business structures, you can run your e-commerce project under the same company/software house as one of its products or brands.

For example:

- Company/Software House Name: `YourTech Solutions`
- Product/Brand 1: `BazarAF`
- Product/Brand 2: `School Management System`
- Service Line: `Website and App Development Services`

This means the software house is the main legal/business entity, and BazarAF is a product operated by that company.

## 3. Do You Need Separate Government Registration for the Software House and E-Commerce Project?

This depends on Afghan business law, your registration type, tax requirements, and whether BazarAF operates as a separate commercial marketplace.

In many cases, you may be able to do one of these:

### Option A: One Registered Company, Multiple Activities

You register one company as an IT/software/e-commerce/digital services company and operate BazarAF under it.

Possible structure:

```text
Registered Company: YourTech Solutions
Business Activities: Software development, IT services, e-commerce platform operations, digital services
Product/Trade Name: BazarAF
```

This is usually simpler and cheaper at the beginning.

### Option B: Software House Registered Separately, E-Commerce Registered Separately

You register the software house as one company and BazarAF as another company.

This may be useful later if:

- BazarAF gets investors separately.
- BazarAF becomes large.
- You want separate accounting.
- You want to sell shares in BazarAF only.
- You want to protect the software house from marketplace liabilities.

### Option C: One Company With Registered Brand/Trade Name

You register the software house company and register BazarAF as a brand/trade name under the same company.

This can be a good middle option if available locally.

## 4. Recommended Legal Structure for Your Situation

Because you are starting with limited money, the practical approach may be:

### Short-Term Recommendation

Start with one company/software house and run BazarAF as a product under it.

Why:

- Lower registration cost
- Easier accounting
- Easier management
- Good for early stage
- Allows service work and product work together

### Long-Term Recommendation

When BazarAF grows, receives investment, or becomes operationally large, consider separating it legally.

Why:

- Cleaner investor structure
- Easier financial reporting
- Separate risk management
- Easier future partnerships
- Easier sale or spin-off

## 5. Important Legal Questions to Ask Locally

Before registration, ask a local lawyer/accountant/government office:

- Can one company legally provide software services and operate an e-commerce marketplace?
- Can I include multiple business activities in one license?
- Can BazarAF be registered as a trade name/brand under the software house?
- What taxes apply to software services?
- What taxes apply to marketplace commissions?
- Do I need a separate e-commerce license?
- Do I need a physical office address?
- What documents are required for registration?
- What are the annual renewal fees?
- What invoices/receipts are required?
- How should I handle seller commissions and payouts?

## 8. Can the Software House Help BazarAF?

Yes. A software house can support BazarAF in many ways.

Benefits:

- Client work can fund BazarAF development.
- BazarAF can be your portfolio product.
- Developers working on client projects can also improve internal products.
- The software house can attract partners and investors.
- BazarAF can later become a separate startup if it grows.

Recommended structure:

```text
Software House = Main company for income and services
BazarAF = Internal product/startup under the company
```

## 9. Risks of Running Both Together

Running both is possible, but be careful.

Risks:

- Client work may take time away from BazarAF.
- BazarAF may consume money from software house income.
- Accounting can become confusing.
- Investors may want BazarAF separate.
- Team focus may become divided.

Solutions:

- Keep separate budgets.
- Track BazarAF expenses separately.
- Track software house revenue separately.
- Use separate project management boards.
- Assign dedicated time for BazarAF.
- Prepare to separate BazarAF legally later.

Your software house should have standards.

## 23. Final Recommendation

Start with a hybrid software house.

Use the software house to generate income from client work and use BazarAF as your flagship internal product.

Recommended path:

1. Start lean from home or small shared office.
2. Build your portfolio around BazarAF.
3. Offer website and custom software services.
4. Register one company when ready.
5. Run BazarAF under the company initially.
6. Keep BazarAF accounting and operations separate internally.
7. Separate BazarAF legally later if it grows or receives investment.

## 24. Simple Final Answer

Yes, you can likely run both the software house and BazarAF together under one company at the beginning, if local registration rules allow your company activity to include software services and e-commerce platform operations.

But for serious investment or large-scale marketplace operations, BazarAF may later need separate legal registration or at least separate accounting and brand registration.
