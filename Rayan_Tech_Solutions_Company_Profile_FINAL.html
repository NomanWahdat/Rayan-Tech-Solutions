Who We Are
About Us
Rayan Tech Solutions is a dynamic software house based in Kabul, Afghanistan. We specialize in delivering tailored digital products that help local and international businesses grow — from e-commerce platforms and custom web applications to comprehensive business management systems and cross-platform mobile apps. Our team combines technical depth with a deep understanding of the Afghan market to build solutions that are both globally competitive and locally relevant.
Our Mission
To empower Afghan businesses with world-class technology solutions that were once accessible only to large corporations. We bridge the gap between ambition and execution by delivering reliable, scalable, and beautifully crafted software that generates measurable business outcomes. Every project we take on is a commitment — to quality, to deadlines, and to the long-term success of our clients. We measure our success by the growth and impact we enable for the businesses we serve.
Vision & Core Values
To become the most trusted technology partner in Afghanistan and the wider region — recognized for innovation, accountability, and transformative impact on businesses of every scale. We build with purpose and deliver with integrity.

Key Projects & Achievements
01
BazarAF – E-Commerce Marketplace
A full-scale multi-vendor marketplace built for the Afghan market. The platform includes seller onboarding, OTP-based authentication, dedicated admin and seller dashboards, complete order management, and a cash-on-delivery payment flow — deployed to production on Vercel.
Django React PostgreSQL Vercel Production Ready
02
Rayan Tech – Corporate Website
A professional multilingual website available in English, Pashto, and Dari. It features a fully animated interface, a service and portfolio showcase, and complete mobile responsiveness — built to represent Rayan Tech Solutions to both local and international audiences.
React TypeScript Tailwind CSS i18next Multilingual
03
School Management System
A comprehensive platform covering student records, daily attendance tracking, grading and report cards, and parent-teacher communication. Role-based access for administrators, teachers, and parents ensures each user sees only what is relevant to them.
Django PostgreSQL REST API Admin Dashboard Enterprise
04
Pharmacy & Healthcare Management
A specialized system for pharmacies and medical facilities that handles prescription tracking, inventory management, supplier integration, and patient history. Designed for operational accuracy and compliance, with real-time data synchronization across all modules.
React Node.js MongoDB Real-time Sync Healthcare
